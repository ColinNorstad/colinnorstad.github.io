Case study

Running operations for ~40 trips a semester

As Basecamp Manager, I run operations with the director for a 50-member co-op that sends out about 40 trips and 250 participants a semester.

Role
Basecamp Manager & Trip Leader
Timeline
Dec 2023 to present
Where
Boston College Outdoor Adventure
Scale
50 members, ~40 trips, ~250 participants a semester

The problem

Boston College's Outdoor Adventure is a 50-member co-op that sends students into the backcountry. It sends out about 40 trips and 250 participants a semester. Before each trip leaves, its leaders need a risk briefing, a place on the calendar, and food.

What I run

As Basecamp Manager, I run operations with the director.

  • Pre-trip briefings: I brief each leader team before departure on risk management, group dynamics, and backup plans.
  • Trip calendar: I own it for the whole co-op.
  • Food procurement: I own it for every trip.
  • Issues: I resolve them as they arise.

Who it serves

Leader teams, and the roughly 250 participants a semester who go out with them. I also plan and lead my own backpacking trips for groups of 6+ students. I'm leading a trip to Iceland over Easter 2027.

Climbing wall and gear rentals

Alongside Outdoor Adventure, I work for BC Campus Recreation (Aug 2024 to present). I supervise climbing wall operations, enforce safety protocols, and give orientations to new climbers. I also run outdoor gear rentals and advise students on gear selection, destinations, and safety.

Scale

  • 50-member co-op
  • ~40 trips a semester
  • ~250 participants a semester

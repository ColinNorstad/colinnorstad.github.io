About

Hi, I'm Colin.

Bay Area native, Boston College senior, and someone who has taken things apart since before I could explain why.

Why engineering

I was taking things apart before I could explain why.

Almost before I could walk I had a TV remote in my hands, and it didn't take long before I was pulling them apart. Not to break them, but to understand them. That urge to see inside a thing and work out what makes it tick never left.

I'm a senior at Boston College, double majoring in Human-Centered Engineering and Computer Science. I chose human-centered engineering because it ties a technical degree to the people who use what you build. I added computer science because most production problems now sit between hardware and software.

That is the work I do. At Tandem PV I built an inventory kiosk, a 20-node sensor network, and custom parts for a solar pilot line. At Blueprint Medicines I built the dashboard a sourcing team used to see its suppliers in one place. In both, I started by asking the people who would use the system what they needed.

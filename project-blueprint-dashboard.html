Case study

Global CDMO spend dashboard

Built a centralized Power BI dashboard that consolidates spend, contracts, and quality events across the CMC supply chain, then demoed it live to senior leadership ahead of an acquisition integration.

Role
Data Visualization Intern
Timeline
Summer 2025
Where
Blueprint Medicines (Sanofi), Cambridge, MA
Tools
Power BI, Power Query

The problem

Blueprint's CMC Strategic Sourcing team worked with contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs) around the world, and the data about them lived across several systems. Spend, contracts, and quality events weren't visible in one place, and transparency mattered more than ever ahead of an acquisition integration.

What I built

A centralized Power BI dashboard that brings global CDMO spend, contracts, and quality events into a single source of truth for the team.

  • Scoped with the people who use it: I worked directly with subject matter experts to decide which questions the dashboard had to answer.
  • Cleaned and integrated: I cleaned and combined complex datasets from multiple systems.
  • Iterated in the open: I presented to cross-functional teams as it took shape, then delivered a live demo to senior leadership.

Making it last

A dashboard that nobody maintains stops being useful, so I wrote a full system maintenance plan documenting roles, refresh cycles, and ownership. It was designed to keep working after my internship ended.

What I learned

Real data is always messier than it looks. The hardest part wasn't building the dashboard but earning stakeholders' trust and learning the business well enough to ask the right questions. Sitting in on acquisition strategy meetings also showed me how supply-chain leaders weigh big decisions.
